Track your 30x30

Dubai Fitness Challenge 2026 · 1–30 November

How it works

  • Hit either daily goal: 10,000 steps or 30 minutes of activity.
  • Every day you hit a goal earns a Day Finisher badge.
  • Collect all 30 to unlock the DFC Finisher badge.

Dates and timing

  • The challenge runs from 1 to 30 November 2026.
  • Each day runs from 12:00am to 11:59pm, Dubai time.
  • You can log activity up to 7 days late. After that, a missed day can't be recovered.

Tracking your activity

  • Steps and active minutes sync automatically from your connected wearable.
  • No wearable? Log workouts yourself — they count exactly the same.

Leaderboards and scoring

  • Organisations are ranked by DFC score, out of 100.
  • DFC score combines participation — how many colleagues have joined and are active — with completion, how many daily goals your organisation hits.
  • You also appear on your team leaderboard, ranked by days completed.

Fair play

  • Track honestly. STEPPI monitors for unusual activity.
  • Workouts you log yourself are capped at 300 minutes a day.
  • Any manipulation may lead to disqualification, after review with your programme manager.
